## Dynamical Transition in a Two-Dimensional Glass Model

**BSc thesis in Physics · Sapienza University of Rome · 2025**  
Leonardo Flavio Iacomelli · Supervised by Matteo Paoluzzi

*Transizione dinamica in un modello di vetro in due dimensioni*

How does a disordered material relax as it cools? For my thesis, I implemented a Metropolis Monte Carlo simulation of a two-dimensional glass-forming mixture and analysed how its structure and dynamics changed with temperature.

### Method

- Simulated a 50:50 mixture of 400 soft particles of two sizes, with periodic boundary conditions.
- Used linked cells to restrict interaction calculations to nearby particles and averaged dynamical observables over 100 independent runs per temperature.

### Results

- Cooling increased the relaxation time measured from the self-intermediate scattering function and prolonged the plateau in mean squared displacement. This plateau marks the interval in which particles are temporarily trapped by their neighbours.
- The radial distribution function showed stronger short-range structure on cooling, with no evidence of long-range crystalline order in the simulated conditions.
- After a rapid quench to a reduced temperature of 0.5, the energy continued to drift, indicating slow equilibration with local Monte Carlo moves.
