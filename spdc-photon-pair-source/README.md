# Characterization of a Non-Degenerate SPDC Photon-Pair Source

**Experimental quantum optics · Physics Laboratory II · 2026**  
Leonardo Flavio Iacomelli

I characterised a source of correlated photon pairs based on spontaneous parametric down-conversion (SPDC). A 396 nm continuous-wave laser pumps a periodically poled KTP crystal, producing one visible photon near 532 nm and one telecom-wavelength photon near 1550 nm per pair.

## Measurements

- Scanned the crystal temperature and identified the quasi-phase-matching point near 36 °C from the peak in the coincidence rate.
- Measured a coincidence rate of 70 kHz after optimising the alignment and fibre coupling. The rate was approximately 63.2 kHz after subtracting estimated accidental coincidences.
- Estimated a generation rate of 1.34 × 10⁷ pairs/s in the collected modes before losses in the detection arms.
